DAA Course Syllabus (2026–27)

  • Unit 1: Introduction to Algorithms & Complexity Analysis
  • Unit 2: Divide and Conquer (Quick Sort, Merge Sort, Strassen's Algorithm)
  • Unit 3: Greedy Method (Kruskal's MST, Prim's Algorithm)
  • Unit 4: Dynamic Programming (LCS, TSP, Knapsack)
  • Unit 5: Graph Algorithms (Dijkstra's, Bellman-Ford)
  • Unit 6: Backtracking & Branch and Bound (N-Queens)
